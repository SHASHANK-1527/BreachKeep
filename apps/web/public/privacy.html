BreachKeep Privacy Policy

BreachKeep is a learning platform used for the Cyber eLabs cybersecurity course. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you sign up we store your username, email address, and a securely hashed password. If you use "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. We do not receive your Google password or access to any other Google data. We also store your course progress, such as which challenges you have completed and the house you were sorted into.

How we use it

Your information is used only to run the course: to let you log in, send you verification and daily access codes by email, and save your progress. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies

We use a small number of essential cookies to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time from your account page, which removes your personal information and progress. You can also email us to request deletion.
