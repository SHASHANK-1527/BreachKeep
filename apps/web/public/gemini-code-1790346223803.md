# Page 3 Design Specification

**1. General Layout & Background**
*   **Background Visual:** A mystical, Asian-inspired night landscape. It features traditional red torii gates in the background, glowing stone lanterns in the foreground, and floating red autumn leaves. The ground is formed by dark, rugged rocks shrouded in mist.
*   **Color Palette:** Deep, rich navy blues and purples in the sky, contrasted with striking crimson reds and golds on the central object and environment.

**2. Core Visual Element**
*   **Centerpiece:** A highly detailed, vibrant red, box-like structure or door resting on the central rocky pedestal. It features a fearsome demon or oni face, gold horns, an ornate tiled roof with golden fish ornaments, thick decorative ropes, and is wrapped in heavy iron chains with large hanging bells.

**3. UI Components**
*   **Header Banner:** Centered near the top of the screen is an ornate, dark blue rectangular banner with a thin gold border. It contains the text "INTRODUCTION" in white, flanked by small diamond-shaped decorative accents.
*   **User Avatar:** Positioned in the top-left corner is a circular, gold-toned avatar icon depicting a user silhouette. *Note: Map this element's `src` directly to the `assets/` folder.*
