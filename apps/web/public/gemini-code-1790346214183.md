# Page 1 Design Specification

**1. General Layout & Background**
*   **Background Visual:** A 2D illustrated night scene featuring a large, bright full moon on the left side. The environment includes a ruined landscape with gravestones on the left, a ruined archway in the center, and a silhouetted castle on the right.
*   **Color Palette:** Dominated by cool tones, primarily shades of blue, ranging from deep midnight blue in the sky to lighter, icy blues near the moon and ground mist.

**2. UI Components**
*   **Central Modal Container:** A prominent, centered, rectangular card with rounded corners. It uses a glassmorphism effect (semi-transparent with a blurred background) to allow the moonlight and ruins to show through.
*   **Heading:** The word "Code" is centered at the top of the modal. It features a thin, dark underline spanning just under the text.
*   **Input Field:** A light grey, rounded rectangular input field is positioned below the heading. It contains the placeholder text "Enter your code".
*   **Action Button:** A smaller, rounded rectangular button labeled "Next" is centered below the input field.
